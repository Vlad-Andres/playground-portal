# Iteration 6 — Package R: Run the Beta and Decide

> Owner-led. An agent can prepare the documents and summarise the numbers.

### R1: Before inviting
- One invite code per beta group (admin), `monthlyJobLimit` set per group.
- A one-page cost sheet in `notes/`: model cost per published course from the G2 runs, storage, hosting, and the monthly ceiling the owner accepts.
- A short interview script (`notes/2026-11-beta-interview.md`): what exam are you preparing for; what did you do first; where was the course wrong or unhelpful; did you share it, with whom; did you come back, why; what would you pay for, and how much.

### R2: Two to three weeks of beta
- Invite 20–30 students across the groups. Watch at least 10 sessions live or on a call.
- Weekly: read admin numbers (F3) and reports (D4); fix blocking bugs in small PRs.

### R3: Decision review
Written to `notes/2026-12-beta-review.md`, with counts and denominators:
- Generation success rate and cost per published course.
- Courses opened again on a later day (returning use).
- Share copies and opens of shared courses by other people.
- Which features were used (study next, due today, exam, Ask AI).
- Interview answers on value and price.

Then choose: (a) open public creation (old launch PR cards 03–05, 08–10), (b) more quality work, (c) a paid plan test, or (d) stop. TikTok / community marketing starts only after (a) is chosen, using the demo-course recordings.
